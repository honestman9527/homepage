## Motion with intent

A paragraph about using animation to explain state — where something came
from, what just changed — instead of decorating the page. Entrance
animations on scroll, collapsible navigation, hover affordances.

## The restraint checklist

- One easing curve family for the whole site
- Durations under 500ms for UI transitions
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
